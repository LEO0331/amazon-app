This is a fictional demonstration memory, not a record of a real family's summer. The imagined worktable holds a folded wallet, a market tote, and a small pouch at different stages of use. Their shapes and wear tell a quieter story together than any one object could tell alone.

The note keeps that shared setting in the archive after the table has been cleared. It leaves room for a real public memory to describe a place or routine without assigning an exact day to it.
