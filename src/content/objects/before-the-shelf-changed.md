This is a fictional demonstration memory, with no real family history behind it. In the imagined arrangement, a photography book stands beside a used film camera, while a postcard rests against the shelf. The three objects share a place for a while, even though each has a separate record.

Shelves change as objects are read, moved, or passed on. This note preserves one possible arrangement without claiming a precise date or owner.
